# Fatti scrivere il prompt lungo

Fonte: Tyler Mommsen

**Come si usa:** incollalo in una chat normale di Claude, compila la parte tra parentesi, poi porta il risultato in `motion-studio`.

```text
I want to make a motion graphics video with Claude Code. Here's what it is: [describe the video, the product or topic, and roughly how long it should be]. Write me a detailed prompt for it with these sections: PRODUCT (or TOPIC), STORY, FORMAT, LOOK, MOTION, SOUND, QUALITY CHECK and RULES. Be specific: exact hex colors, named fonts, how each scene turns into the next, what the camera does, and what sound plays on each action. In QUALITY CHECK, tell Claude to render it, look at a frame every half second, critique it like a senior motion designer, fix everything and repeat for at least four rounds. Avoid anything that looks like a template.
```
